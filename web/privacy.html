Family Circle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Family Circle is a private social app for families. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Family Circle is operated by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] (contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]).

What we collect

  • Account details: your first name, family name and email address, and your password (held securely by Google Firebase Authentication - we never see it).
  • Profile: the short bio and profile picture (emoji) you choose.
  • Things you add: posts, comments, reactions, private and group messages, calendar events, to-do lists and contacts (names, phone numbers, addresses and notes you type in).
  • Notification settings and device tokens: so we can send alerts to your phone or browser.
  • Calendar sync (optional): if you choose to connect Google or Outlook calendar, the app reads your events to show them on your private calendar. They are read-only, held only in memory while you use the app, and are not stored on our servers.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Your circle (family group) is private. Only approved members of your circle can see its feed, family calendar and shared contacts.
  • Private messages are visible only to the people in that conversation.
  • Private calendar events, private contacts and to-do lists are visible only to you, unless you choose to share them.
  • Circle administrators can approve or remove members and moderate posts in their own circle.
  • Different circles are kept completely separate from each other.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging and Hosting), which processes it on our behalf under Google's privacy terms. Data is encrypted in transit.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and personal data (see below). Posts or messages you shared with other members may be removed or anonymised.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time by emailing [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Family Circle account". We will confirm once it is done, normally within 30 days. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it.

Children

Family Circle is intended for families. Accounts for children should be created and supervised by a parent or guardian, who is responsible for what is shared. We do not knowingly collect information from children without a parent's or guardian's involvement.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell members in the app.
