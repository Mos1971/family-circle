Delete your Family Circle account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Family Circle and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

If you are the only admin of a circle that still has other members, make another member an admin first (Admin dashboard), then delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password) and account record.
  • Your profile in every circle you belong to.
  • Your posts, comments, messages, calendar entries, contacts and to-do lists.
  • Your notifications and the device tokens used for push alerts.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Can't open the app?

Email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you registered with, subject "Delete my Family Circle account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.
