# Google Play listing text (draft)

**App name:** Family Circle

**Short description (80 max):**
Private feed, chat, calendar and contacts for your family.

**Full description:**
Family Circle is a private, ad-free space just for your family. Share news and photos-worth moments in a family feed, message one-to-one or in groups, keep a shared calendar and address book, and share to-do lists.

- Family feed: post, comment and react. See at a glance who liked a post.
- Private and group messages that only the people in the chat can read.
- Shared family calendar plus your own private calendar, with start and end dates and times.
- Shared contacts: names, numbers, addresses and notes, with one-tap call and directions. Keep any contact private to you, or share it with the family.
- To-do lists you can share with the people you choose.
- Announcements from your circle's admins and alerts when something happens.
- Join as many circles as you like (your family, your wider family, a club) and switch between them.
- Five colour schemes with light and dark modes, chosen to be easy to read.
- You are in control: new members are approved by the circle admin, and you can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

No adverts. We never sell your data.
